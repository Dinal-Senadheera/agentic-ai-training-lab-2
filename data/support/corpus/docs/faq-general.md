# Frequently asked questions

**Can I keep selling when the internet is down?**
Yes. Offline mode stores up to 500 sales and syncs them later.

**How long do loyalty points last?**
Points expire 12 months after they are earned.

**Which receipt printers can I use?**
Only Epson printers are supported.

**Can customers get refunds?**
Yes, within the refund window. Large refunds need a manager PIN.
