# Refunds policy

This policy applies to every merchant unless their contract addendum says otherwise.

- The standard refund window is **14 days** from the date of sale.
- Refunds above **LKR 25,000** need a manager PIN.
- Card refunds go back to the original card only.
- Cash refunds are limited to LKR 50,000 per terminal per day.
- Refunds outside the refund window are not possible from the till. The merchant may
  issue store credit at their own discretion.
