# Offline mode in Aralia POS Cloud (4.x)

Cloud tills cache sales in the browser or device when the internet drops.

- A till can hold up to **2,000 transactions** or **72 hours** of offline trading,
  whichever comes first. After that, new sales are blocked until the till reconnects.
- **Store-and-forward card payments** are available on the Franchise and Premium plans
  only. Each offline card payment is limited to **LKR 15,000**. The card is charged when
  the till reconnects.
- On Starter and Standard plans, card payments are disabled while offline.
- Offline sales sync automatically within a few minutes of reconnecting.
