# Card and QR payments overview

Aralia Pay processes card and QR payments for Cloud merchants using the Aralia Pay R2
card reader.

Processing fees, settlement times and the chargeback fee are set by finance and published
in the signed fee schedule (img-fee-schedule, scanned copy, effective 1 July 2026). Do not
quote fees from memory; always check the current schedule.

Payouts go to the merchant's nominated bank account. Merchants can see each payout in
Reports > Payouts.
