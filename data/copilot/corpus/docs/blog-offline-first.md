# Why we built Aralia offline-first

Internet connections in many towns still drop during monsoon storms. A till that stops
working when the connection drops is a till that loses sales. That is why offline mode
has always been at the heart of Aralia. Offline sales, offline receipts, offline loyalty:
our customers trade through power cuts, router restarts and patchy mobile data.

Offline-first is a promise to every shopkeeper, from a single boutique in Nuwara Eliya to
a supermarket chain in Colombo, that the till keeps working. Talk to our sales team to
find the right plan for your offline needs.
